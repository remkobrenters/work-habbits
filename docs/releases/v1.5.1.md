# Work Habbits v1.5.1

This release simplifies the hand-break countdown in the top bar and makes a one-minute rest reset both hand timers at any point.

## Changes

- Show one **Hands** indicator for whichever short or long hand break comes next. Due and overdue breaks take priority over upcoming or snoozed breaks.
- Reset both hand timers after one continuous minute without typing or clicking, even before a reminder is due. A 20-second rest still completes only the short break.
- Keep separate short and long hand-break cards in the menu, and update the settings, help text, and rendered previews to match.
- Replace the configurable long-break rest duration with the fixed one-minute reset. Short-break rest remains configurable from 10 to 60 seconds, with a 20-second default.

## Upgrade

Run `./install.sh` as your normal user. History and reminder intervals are preserved. Both hand timers now reset after one minute of rest regardless of the previous long-break duration setting. The updated extension loads at your next login.
