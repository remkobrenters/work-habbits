# Work Habbits v1.4.0

This release adds separate short and long hand break reminders.

## Changes

- Short hand breaks default to 20 seconds of rest after 20 minutes of active keyboard or mouse input. They have their own progress bar, score, and configurable timing. Short reminders cannot be snoozed.
- Long hand breaks default to five minutes of rest after 60 minutes of active input. Five minutes without typing or clicking completes the break, including time spent idle. Long reminders retain their configurable snooze.
- The top bar and menu show separate Short hands and Long hands progress. Completing a long break also completes a short break that is due.
- Both timers and their outcomes survive extension restarts. Existing history and settings remain readable.

## Upgrade

Run `./install.sh` as your normal user. The updated extension loads at your next login. Configure both break types in Settings. Existing custom long hand timing settings are preserved; the new long defaults apply to installations that used the old defaults.

## Validation

The Shell check covers native menu rendering, permission explanation, input summaries, Bluetooth status display, manual posture, and pause/resume. Physical input and the administrator permission dialog still require a desktop check.
