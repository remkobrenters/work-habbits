# Work Habbits v1.7.0

Standing and marked movement breaks now have a configurable minimum duration. Start whenever it suits you and stay standing or walking as long as you like.

## Changes

- Reach the minimum, 20 minutes by default, to reset your seated timer. Finishing a break sooner resumes the previous seated timer.
- A break that starts before the seated reminder turns red earns one habit point at the minimum, even if you start before the reminder is due.
- The top bar shows **Done** when you reach the minimum. You can continue your break penalty-free. The sit-down warning and its snooze setting have been removed.

## Upgrade

Run `./install.sh` as your normal user. The updated extension loads at your next login. Your configured standing duration becomes the minimum, and your saved history is kept.
